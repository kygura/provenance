About

Doge is a slang term for "dog" that is primarily associated with pictures of Shiba Inus and internal monologue captions on Tumblr. These photos may be photoshopped to change the dog's face or captioned with interior monologues in Comic Sans font.

Origin

The use of the misspelled word "doge" to refer to a dog dates back to June 24th, 2005, when it was mentioned in an episode of Homestar Runner's puppet show. The 2010 photograph of Kabosu, a rescued Shiba Inu owned by Japanese kindergarten teacher Atsuko Sato, became the canonical image. The meme reached critical mass in late 2013 on Reddit and Tumblr.

Spread

On December 6th, 2013, software engineers Billy Markus and Jackson Palmer launched Dogecoin, a cryptocurrency using the meme as its mascot. The meme resurfaced during the January 2021 retail trading wave, and again in 2023 when the Doge image briefly replaced the Twitter logo. Each revival introduced the meme to a cohort that had not seen the previous one.
